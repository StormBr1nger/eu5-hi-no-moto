# Vision & Identity

## Project Name

**Hi no Moto: Realm of the Rising Sun**

Short title:

**Hi no Moto**

## Meaning

**Hi no Moto** is inspired by the Japanese expression connected to “the origin/source of the sun,” often associated with Japan as the land where the sun rises.

## Core Fantasy

The player participates in the long transformation of Japan through clan rivalry, imperial legitimacy, shogunate power, regional fragmentation, unification, consolidation, foreign pressure, and alternative historical paths.

## Historical Arc

```text
Nanbokuchō legacy
↓
Muromachi / Ashikaga order
↓
Sengoku fragmentation
↓
Unification wars
↓
Edo-style consolidation
↓
Westernization / foreign pressure
↓
Alternative history:
Imperial restoration, rival shogunates, clan-led Japan
```

## Project Identity

- Historical inspiration, not strict railroading
- Japan-centered strategic depth
- Political legitimacy matters as much as conquest
- Clan identity and regional power are core
- Alternative outcomes should feel plausible
