# Design Pillars

## 1. History Inspires. Gameplay Decides.

The project should be historically grounded, but not locked into one fixed outcome.

## 2. Legitimacy Is Power.

Military control alone should not be enough. Authority, court recognition, titles, prestige, and tradition should matter.

## 3. Clans Are Political Machines.

Clans should not be simple countries. They should represent family power, vassal networks, rival branches, retainers, and local influence.

## 4. The Realm Is Fragmented.

Japan should feel politically layered: emperor, court, shogunate, clans, provinces, temples, cities, and foreign powers.

## 5. Unification Is Not the Only Story.

Survival, restoration, regional dominance, trade power, religious authority, or alternative political orders should also be valid paths.
